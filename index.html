New to boating? What is a transom angle?

The transom is the flat wall at the back of the boat where the engine is mounted. It is not perfectly vertical: it leans back, usually between 12° and 15°. An outboard bracket moves the engine back, behind the boat. Its degree rating is made to match the transom so the engine hangs straight.

Enthusiast notes

A bracket that does not match the transom leaves the engine tilted from the start, which changes how much trim range you have in each direction. Take the reading in more than one spot and compare. If your numbers differ, or you are outside 11°–15°, tell us when you request a quote and we can build a custom bracket.
